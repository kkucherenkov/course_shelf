## Dependencies

## Spec / Design references

- [ADR-0013](../../adr/0013-ownership-taxonomy-and-ratings.md) — decision 1:
  enrollment is its own entity, separate from `AccessGrant`

## Goal

Let a learner say "I am taking this" before the first play, and stop the
product inferring it from `progress > 0`.

## Acceptance

- `Enrollment(userId, courseId, startedAt)`, unique on `(userId, courseId)`
- Enroll and unenroll routes, plus the requester's enrollment state on the
  course detail response
- Access is untouched: enrolling neither grants nor requires an `AccessGrant`,
  and a course can still be opened without enrolling
- Unenrolling deletes the enrollment and nothing else — progress, notes and
  bookmarks survive

## Spec diff

openapi.yaml — enrollment routes, and an enrollment flag on the course detail
response

## Codegen impact

yes

## Design impact

no

## Tests

Handler specs for enroll, idempotent re-enroll, unenroll, and unenroll of
something never enrolled. An access-control test proving enrollment is
orthogonal to the grant.
