## Goal

Four tables and two enums: the transcript index that doubles as the skip marker, its cues, the run, and the run's per-lesson errors.

## Acceptance

- `Transcript(lessonId, language, origin, sourcePath, sourceMtime, sourceSize, derivedPath)` unique on `(lessonId, language)`
- `TranscriptCue(transcriptId, startMs, endMs, text)`
- `Transcription` + `TranscriptionErrorRecord`, shaped like `Scan` + `ScanErrorRecord`
- **No foreign key** from `transcript` to `lesson` or `subtitle`
