## Notes

- The thumbnails-on-a-read-only-mount limit is **not** in this epic: it is fixed by [E25-F04-S01](./E25-F04-S01.md), which introduces the writable derived volume it needs.
- **Settled: kept, not removed.** The maintainer ran the Downloads screen on a
  physical device on 2026-08-30 and the bar reported plausible free/used
  figures. Recorded by what means deliberately: "verified" without saying how is
  the kind of claim this epic exists to stop.
- Covered by two widget tests in
  `apps/mobile/test/features/downloads/disk_space_device_storage_test.dart`,
  driving `StorageBar` from what a stubbed `disk_space_plus` returned — so the
  megabyte→byte conversion and the plugin-throws degrade path are asserted
  against the rendered caption, not against a hand-built `StorageSnapshot`.
- **What this does not cover:** the stub is the plugin's Dart interface. The
  native Android/iOS implementations behind it are still unexercised in CI, and
  stay tracked as issue #280. The device run above is a one-off by a human, not
  a gate.
