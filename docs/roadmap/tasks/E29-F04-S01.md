## Goal

A lesson's transcript and its slides become a draft you would keep, generated
on demand and reviewed before it counts.

## Acceptance

- One ffmpeg command per lesson finds and writes candidate frames in a single
  pass (`select='gt(scene,0.4)',showinfo,scale=1280:-2`), with a parser pairing
  each `pts_time:` line to the file it wrote
- Caps hold: at most 24 candidates reach the vision call, at most 8 survive
  into the summary
- A vision pass drops frames carrying no information and writes the alt text
  for those that stay; the summary call never sees a filename and never invents
  a timestamp
- `LessonSummary` carries `status` (proposed / applied / discarded), the model
  that produced it and an immutable `bodyMd`; a second generation adds another
  `proposed` row rather than replacing the first, and at most one row per lesson
  is `applied`
- Frames sit in the body as `![](frame:<frameId>)`, written by our code after
  the vision pass, so one body renders as a signed URL in the admin preview and
  as `images/NNN.jpg` in the export
- A placeholder whose frame row is gone renders as nothing, so a deleted frame
  cannot break an export
- Generation is admin-only and on demand, locked per course by
  `QuizGenerationLockService`; export stays open to any user who can see the
  lesson
- A lesson with no transcript answers 409 rather than summarising from nothing
- Admin review screen: accept a draft or discard it, the `Quiz` propose /
  apply / discard lifecycle verbatim

## Spec diff

openapi.yaml — `POST /lessons/{id}/summaries`, `POST /courses/{id}/summaries`,
`GET /lessons/{id}/summaries`, `POST /summaries/{id}/apply`,
`POST /summaries/{id}/discard`, and the signed
`GET /stream/summaries/{id}/frames/{frameId}` mirroring the poster route.
Land them with their controller in the same PR — a path with no controller
behind it cannot satisfy the Schemathesis contract test.

## Notes

- Carved out of `T-2026-09-17-lesson-summary-export` on 2026-09-22. The design
  pre-step, ADR-0012 and the hosted model adapter shipped there; the export half
  shipped as E28-F01-S01 in PR #766. This card is the remainder, deferred rather
  than cancelled, which is why it is Stage A: the product decisions it needed
  are already settled in the design doc.
- Cost against the library as it stood (68 courses, 5973 lessons, 1952
  transcripts): $0.0007 a lesson, $0.03 a course, $1.30 for every existing
  transcript. CPU is the constraint, not money — scene detection decodes the
  whole video, so a 49-lesson course occupies ffmpeg for 20 to 40 minutes.
- Out of scope, per the design: editing a summary in the app, summaries on
  mobile, regenerating one section, and any summary in a language other than
  the transcript's.
