## Spec / Design references

- [ADR-0013](../../adr/0013-ownership-taxonomy-and-ratings.md) — decision 3: a
  rating is personal, and the source's rating stays what it is

## Goal

Rate a course without overwriting the number Stepik or Coursera shipped with it.

## Acceptance

- `CourseRating(userId, courseId, stars)`, unique on `(userId, courseId)`;
  set, change and clear
- `Course.ratingAverage` and `Course.ratingCount` keep meaning the source's
  number — nothing in this card writes them
- Card and course page show both, labelled so they cannot be read as one number
- `listCourses` sorting offers both: the source's rating and mine

## Spec diff

openapi.yaml — a rating route, the requester's rating on the course
responses, and two new `sort` values

## Codegen impact

yes

## Design impact

no

## Tests

Handler specs for set, overwrite and clear; a query spec for each new sort
value, including where a course carries one number and not the other.
