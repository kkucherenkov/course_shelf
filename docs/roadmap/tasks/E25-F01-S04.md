## Goal

Speech-to-text behind a port, so the application layer never learns the engine is whisper.cpp, a child process, or local at all.

## Acceptance

- `WhisperAdapter.transcribe({ audioAbsolutePath, outBaseAbsolutePath })` → `{ srtAbsolutePath }`
- `LocalWhisperAdapter` shells out with `execFile`, mirroring `LocalFfmpegAdapter` including the manual promise wrapper and its reason
- Model, threads, language and timeout come from `AppConfig`
- Non-zero exit or timeout raises `WhisperFailedError`
