## Goal

Reach a note written three courses ago without remembering which lesson it was
on.

## Acceptance

- Cross-course list routes for the requester's notes and bookmarks, paginated
- Two groupings, both available: by course and by date
- Every row carries its course title and its lesson title — a bare body is
  unusable out of context
- A bookmark row deep-links to the lesson at its timestamp (`?t=`)
- Strings via `t()` in `en` and `ru`; `pnpm check:i18n` green

## Spec diff

openapi.yaml — two cross-course list routes. Notes and bookmarks are
lesson-scoped in the contract today: `upsertNote`, `getNote` and
`listLessonBookmarks` all take a `lessonId`, so nothing existing can answer
"all of mine".

## Tests

specs for both groupings and the empty state.
