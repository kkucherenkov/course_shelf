## Goal

Read along with the lesson, find a line, and jump to it.

## Acceptance

- A fifth tab beside sections / notes / bookmarks / materials
- The cue under the playhead is highlighted and `aria-current`
- Clicking a cue emits the sidebar's existing `seek` event, in seconds
- A filter box narrows the visible cues, case-insensitively, over the in-memory array
- Empty and no-match states are distinct and translated
- Strings via `t()` in `en` **and** `ru`; `pnpm check:i18n` green
